# ch08 — tells audit (bs14, lane A)

## Header

- **Counts by severity:** charter 1 · tell 20 · noted 10 · total 31
- **Em-dashes:** 82 in prose (code fences and HTML comments stripped), 5831 prose words → **14.1 per 1000** — the lane's highest raw count. Nearly every paragraph carries at least one dash; the one-pair-per-paragraph line is mostly respected, the "rare" line is not.
- **Negation-family count:** 16 instances (all variants: "not X, but Y", "It is not X. It is Y.", "No X, no Y, no Z" anaphora), of which 1 is charter-sanctioned (the freeze/Tristan passage is TONE.md §4.1's own worked placement) → **15 unsanctioned**. Lane maximum.
- **Banned-vocabulary hits:** none. Verified by eye; no delve/crucial/pivotal/vital/robust/etc., no "serves as/stands as/boasts" copulas. Vocabulary discipline is intact — the tells here are rhythmic and structural, not lexical.
- **Verdict:** the chapter's structure, honesty, and code-first discipline are genuinely K&R, but the cadence is not — a heavy overlay of negation anaphora, self-ranking sentences ("the chapter's largest claim"), and reflexive em-dashes keeps the prose one register more performed than Ritchie would sit still for.

## Findings

### Charter

- [1013-1015, 1023-1025, 1035-1037] «the failure mode is a use-after-free at 3 a.m.» / «is a leak with a scope» / «for a reader who would rather not say the word "region" twice» — sass budget: three wry clauses inside one closing section (§8.8, ~2 rendered pages); TONE.md §2 allows at most one wry sentence per page and "most pages have none"; severity: charter; keep "3 a.m." (it carries the distributional argument), demote the other two to plain statement.

### Tell

**Pattern N1 — negative-space anaphora ("No X. No Y. No Z." / "Not A, not B, not C."). Five instances; one chapter signature, one bs15 fix.**

- [42] «Not two frees, not four, not one per object. One.» — negation anaphora + one-word punch sentence; severity: tell; "All of it is freed by one call, not one per object."
- [115-117] «notice what is not in it. No region parameter. No annotation on the return type. No opinion, anywhere in that function, about where its memory comes from» — negation anaphora triplet; severity: tell; "notice that `parse` has no region parameter and no annotation on its return type."
- [134-135] «there is no second one anywhere. Not in the signature, not on the struct, not on the parameter.» — negation anaphora triplet; severity: tell; "there is no second one anywhere — not in the signature, the struct, or the parameter" collapses to one clause.
- [375-377] «No `Option`, no placeholder, no "we will patch this up in a second pass" comment.» — negation anaphora triplet; severity: tell; "with no `Option` and no placeholder."
- [410-411] «There is no `unsafe` in that program, no reference counting, no interior mutability wrapper, and no lifetime.» — negation list, four beats; severity: tell; defensible as a real enumeration of Rust apparatus, but it is the fifth instance of the pattern in one chapter; trim to two items or recast affirmatively.

**Pattern N2 — "It is not X. It is Y." and compact "X, not Y". TONE.md §3 models the construction, so this is class tell, not charter — but five-plus unsanctioned instances in one chapter is drift, not seasoning.**

- [247] «Closed is not dead. Closed means nobody is allocating right now.» — It-is-not-X-It-is-Y + epizeuxis; severity: tell; "A closed region still holds its data; nobody is allocating right now."
- [460-462] «Cyclic data is not an exception the language grudgingly permits. It is what the inside of a region is for.» — negation family, section-punchline position; severity: tell; "The inside of a region is where cyclic data belongs."
- [806-808] «the way to choose between them is not the mechanism. It is the failure contract — what you want to happen on the day the target is gone.» — negation family + em-dash appendage; severity: tell; "choose by the failure contract: what should happen on the day the target is gone."
- [843-844] «A `weak` is not a reference you read — it is a question you ask.» — negation family with em-dash hinge; severity: tell; the aphorism is good enough to survive once — but it lands amid four siblings; "Reading a `weak` means asking whether the value is still there."
- [1010-1018] «The claim wolf makes is not that arenas are faster than malloc; … the comparison that matters is not against C-with-arenas but against the C that actually gets written.» — two negation-family constructions in one paragraph; severity: tell; the argument is honest and should survive — recast one of the two ("the comparison that matters is against the C that actually gets written").
