This edition does not carry this chapter.

`wolf bench diff` is the referee for every performance claim the book
makes: medians and mean absolute deviation, allocation counts, and a
variance gate that calls a three-percent win noise. The instrument
belongs to the toolchain rather than to the book, and the toolchain this
edition names does not ship it. Reading a report the reader cannot
produce is the one thing this book does not do, so the section numbers
are reserved and the pages are not written.
