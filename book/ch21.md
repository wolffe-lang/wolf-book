This edition does not carry this chapter.

The comparisons against C are measurements, and a measurement needs an
instrument. Chapter 20's `wolf bench diff` is that instrument, and this
edition does not carry chapter 20 either. The claims this chapter exists
to test — that safe wolf matches hand-`restrict` C on the same kernel,
that a region beats malloc discipline, that checked arithmetic's real
cost is small and dated — are the book's most falsifiable, and printing
them without CI's numbers behind them would make them advertising. The
section numbers are reserved and the pages are not written.

Where the book does compare against C, it compares programs rather than
speeds: chapters 26, 27, and 28 print a C twin beside each wolf version
and count the lines, and §26.5 and §32.3 say where the C is better.
